# Music & Audio Direction

*Started July 31 2026. Companion to the "Audio direction" entry in
`docs/PLAN.md`. Reference album: Von Hohenheim — "Silicon Soul" (a literal
BLAME! tribute; CC BY-NC-ND, so reference-only unless a separate license is
negotiated with the artist).*

## Palette

Drone ambient, industrial, minimal synth, dark IDM. Mechanical resonance and
decay. Sparse and patient. Silence is an instrument.

## Principles

1. **Audio is another field over space.** Ambient beds keyed to region/biome;
   the curves-over-altitude idea extends to sound — organic drones in the
   depths, cleaner colder tones toward the heights.
2. **The structure is the instrument.** Diegetic sources at machines, vents,
   elevators, pipes — the fixture system already knows where they are.
3. **Sparseness carries it.** Few simultaneous sources, long loops, huge
   atmosphere-per-byte. Light as hell, like everything else here.

## Acquisition paths

- Source artists in the scene (Bandcamp tags: dark ambient, drone,
  industrial, "blame", megastructure, dungeon-synth-adjacent).
- Generate with Suno using the prompts below (or the audio pipeline in
  asset-bot when SFX-scale work starts).
- License the reference album (one flattering email; low odds, zero cost).

## Suno prompts by location

Craft tips: add **"no percussion"** explicitly wherever pure drone is wanted
(Suno loves sneaking beats in), and generate LONG then loop the best 2–3
minute stretch — ambient survives looping far better than melodic music.

### The depths / catacomb-quarry stratum

> Instrumental, dark drone ambient, industrial decay, cavernous reverb,
> sub-bass rumble, distant metallic groans, dripping water, tape hiss,
> glacial pace, no percussion, oppressive, ancient machinery breathing

### The built city / dungeon interiors (default bed)

> Instrumental, minimal synth, drone ambient, cold concrete atmosphere,
> faint electrical hum, slow evolving pads, sparse piano notes with long
> decay, distant ventilation drone, patient, desolate, 60 bpm

### Ember / machine districts

> Instrumental, industrial ambient, furnace roar low in the mix, rhythmic
> mechanical pulse like distant forging, hot metal resonance, dark analog
> synth swells, menacing but hypnotic, slow industrial heartbeat

### The heights / divine ascent (curves-over-altitude payoff)

> Instrumental, ambient drone, ethereal choir pads, crystalline high
> frequencies, vast empty space, shimmering overtones, cold and pure,
> sacred minimalism, weightless, overwhelming light

### Roads district / open streets under gray sky

> Instrumental, dark ambient with minimal IDM elements, soft wind over
> concrete, sparse glitch percussion far away, melancholic synth drones,
> endless horizon feeling, gray, contemplative motion

### Elevator / transitional interstitials (short pieces)

> Instrumental, drone ambient, mechanical cable resonance, deep shaft echo,
> slowly rising pitch, claustrophobic then opening, minimal, 2 minute
> vignette

### The surfacing moment (inside→outside sting)

> Instrumental, ambient, sudden openness after claustrophobia, low drone
> resolving into wide airy pad, single distant tone like a horn across a
> canyon, awe, relief, enormous scale

### Silicon life / creepy set-pieces

> Instrumental, experimental dark ambient, organic wet textures over machine
> drone, dissonant bowed metal, irregular breathing rhythm, unsettling,
> something alive in the walls, horror ambience without jump scares
