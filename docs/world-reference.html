The Layer Pipeline src/game/DungeonGenerator.ts

One level, generated in strict order. Every layer is a pure function of (stackSeed, cellX, cellZ) plus a bounded neighbor radius — negative coordinates are first-class, so a bounded world is just this pipeline evaluated over a window. Grid math: 1 tile = 3 world units · 1 dungeon cell = 14 tiles · 1 pillar cell = 4×4 dungeon cells = 56 tiles · world = 16×16 cells = 224 tiles = 672 wu.

The column model (columns.ts) is the one source of truth: per-(x,z) sorted AIR spans in world Y. Renderer faces, physics, and agents all derive from span differences — a wall face exists exactly where air meets solid between neighbors. If it isn't in the columns, it doesn't exist.

Regions — Districts of the Megastructure src/game/dungeon/region-layer.ts

The coarsest layer: vast tracts (REGION_CELLS 8 dungeon cells ≈ 336 wu per axis, domain-warped ±2.5 cells so borders meander) that DICTATE the biome palette inside them. The wildness/depth fields stay continuous across borders — a district changes the zoning, not the geography. Growing the world = adding region types and palettes here; the biome selector never changes again.

City weight 0.35

Built mass — endless corridors and compressed residential strata. Measured mix: dungeon 43%, crypt 28%, organic intrusions rare.

Machine weight 0.30

The builders' domain — carved voids and heat. Measured: cave 30%, ember 21%, built 42%.

Canyon weight 0.20

Vast open cuts, sky far above, arch country. Measured: outside 31%.

Frontier weight 0.15

Contested edges where everything interleaves — carries the old global thresholds.

Biomes src/game/dungeon/layer2-biome.ts

Derived, not rolled: two smooth fields — wildness (built ↔ organic ↔ open sky) and depth (common ↔ rare variant) — thresholded by the cell's DISTRICT palette, then quantized to one biome per 14-tile cell. Both are 3-octave fractal noise; wildness is domain-warped (±1.6 cells) and mixed 30% with the pillar elevation field. Vertical drift LEVEL_Y_STEP 0.45 makes regions persist as strata between stacked levels. Target mix: dungeon ≈39%, crypt ≈20%, cave ≈17%, outside ≈18%, ember ≈6%.

Pillar Kebabs pillar-layer.ts · pillar-chunks.ts · pillar-geometry.ts

The megastructures. One pillar cell = 56 tiles (168 wu); the pillar occupies the central ~30×30 tiles. A kebab of authored chunks is stacked BOTH WAYS from grade: the UP section to a noise-driven height (MIN 36 – MAX 80 wu), and a DOWN section into the foundation driven by its own smooth field (~45% of pillars stay surface-only; the rest sink 8–32 wu; 15% of deep pillars are wells — nothing above grade but a crown plinth, a spiral descending below). ~18% of cells are void gaps. Every chunk carries a stair flight up one face and the face advances one quarter-turn clockwise per chunk — one continuous spiral from the deepest landing through grade to the crown, never verified, unbreakable by construction. Below-grade chunks carve their air out of the solid foundation (deep clearances, unclamped); the at-grade chunk owns the ground entry flight. Below grade only plain and shaft spawn for now — buried plazas and halls are future meats.

Plain h 8 · weight 3

Solid core (17–38) + the face flight. Nothing else. The connective shaft chunk.

Terrace h 6 · weight 3

Slim waist (21–34) inside a broad flat plaza ring — prime bridge real estate. Plaza omits its band over the arriving flight (stairs climb under open air); colonnade posts on the inner ledge; that face exposes no sockets.

socketsbridge + ledge at y 0.5 on 3 faces (not the ramp face)

Gallery h 12 · weight 2

Hollow interior hall carved in the core; doorway (tiles 26–29, 4 high) on the face opposite the ramp — a bridge can enter the pillar's inside.

socketsbridge at door face y 0.5; interior ledge

Shaft h 13.8 · weight 2

The express meat: stairs without stops. Height 13.8 = 23 treads at the fixed 0.6 rise — exactly the climb between the two corner squares, so the flight fills the whole band and its only flat tiles are the shared corners. (Corners must stay flat: a climbing tread there gets erased by the neighboring flight's headroom punch.)

Residential h 11 · weight 2 · city meat

Kowloon-compressed living strata: 4 stacked floors at 2.5 pitch, 2.0 headroom — cramped by design. Each stratum opens a doorway slit onto the ramp band exactly where the spiral passes its height, so climbing the pillar threads past inhabited floors. 3× likelier in city districts.

Crown h 4 · always last

Solid cap; its top is the rooftop (+CROWN_HEADROOM 4 of standing air). Placed explicitly, weight 0. Ledge sockets on all four faces at roof height.

Footprint Rings pre-rotation

Tiles 14–41 touch only the middle 2×2 of the cell's 4×4 dungeon cells — the outer ring of cells stays free for rooms, spawn, and exit. Foundation runs to −40; terrain flows under the footprint and the foundation rises to meet it per tile.

Connective Systems pillar-bridges.ts · DungeonGenerator.ts

Bridges neighbor-pair pass

Pure function of two adjacent specs + the pair's own seed (derived from the lower coordinate — both sides compute identical results independently). Walkway 2 tiles wide at socket height, sloping if sockets differ; solid slab 0.5 + clearance 3.5 carved into columns. Through air it's a bridge; through solid it's a carved passage.

per pairmax 2, chance 0.65, vertical separation ≥ 10
socket matchΔy ≤ 10 (forced fallback ≤ 24), min height 6
degreeevery pillar guaranteed ≥1 bridge via radius-1 forced pass
pipes35% of chance bridges are enclosed DUCTS: tight 2.6 bore, roofed — sewer-scale pipe crossings. Guarantee bridges stay open walkways.

Ground & Pits layer 6

Grade terrain is always walkable — rolling but never needing a jump. Drama comes from HOLES: a 3D noise field (scale 14, shared across a stack) drops whole areas out of the floor; aligned holes become atria or bottomless pits. The golden path relaxes into a walkable channel (max rise 0.7/tile) and crosses pits as narrow causeways. Spawn/exit stay out of holes (radius 3).

Marriage & Seams the doctrine

Pillar ground surfaces within 1.0 of terrain marry the level: owner 0, written into the height field, propagated across footprints to a fixpoint. Married slabs draw dead flat (structures don't roll with hills), terrain corners bend to meet them, skirts seal flat-vs-blended edges, and stair risers force raw face bounds only on the slab side. Junctions interpenetrate, never abut.

Roofline Cull interior pillars

A nearest-room BFS cap field (lowest roof wins) makes interior pillars pass through building roofs as one solid mass — no chimneys. Ramp clearances (punching) and plaza/interior allow-zones stay open as enclosed passages; the crown attic survives on interior pillars only.
